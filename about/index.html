Welcome

I'm Aayush Agrawal

I'm an undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at BMS College of Engineering in Bangalore, expected to graduate in 2028. I build small, useful software: Python tools, Discord bots and websites.

Thanks for taking the time to check out my portfolio. If you have any questions or want to work together, email me at agrawal.aayush38@gmail.com.

About Me

I like building things from scratch, whether that's a quick Python utility, a bot for a community, or a long dig into how a model actually learns. Most of my projects start as something I wanted for myself, like the Attendance Calculator shortcut that tells me how many classes I can skip.

I'm part of @clienterrverse, where I work on open-source projects with other developers and learn how real-world software gets made. You can find those on my Projects page.

Outside of code

At college I volunteer for BMSCE UTSAV, our fest, and I was a member of the Rotaract Club of BMSCE, doing community service around Bengaluru. When I'm not coding, I'm reading docs, taking things apart to see how they work, and collecting ideas for the next side project, usually with a cold can of White.

Tools I use

Python, JavaScript, Java, C, HTML & CSS, Node.js, Discord.js, MySQL, MongoDB, Machine Learning and Git. I speak English, Hindi and Nepali.
